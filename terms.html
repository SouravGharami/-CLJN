📅 Billing Cycle & Renewal

All CLJN subscription plans are billed on a recurring monthly or annual basis, beginning on the date of initial purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each billing period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

  • Billing occurs on the same calendar date each month or year corresponding to your original subscription activation date.
  • Annual subscriptions are billed in full at the start of each annual term and are not prorated for cancellations made mid-term.
  • Users will receive an email reminder 7 days before each renewal, detailing the upcoming charge amount and renewal date.
  • Price changes will be communicated with a minimum of 30 days' advance notice before taking effect on your account.

⬆ Plan Upgrades & Downgrades

You may upgrade your subscription plan at any time, with changes taking effect immediately. The cost difference will be prorated for the remaining billing period. Downgrading your plan takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and no refund is issued for the difference in the current period.

Minister subscription accounts may be upgraded to include additional consultation slots, premium placement in the minister directory, and advanced analytics dashboards at any time without disruption to existing session bookings.

Section 05

Refund Policy

💰 Subscription Refunds

CLJN operates a fair and transparent refund policy. New subscribers who have not accessed any premium content or booked any sessions may request a full refund within 48 hours of their initial payment. After this window, refunds are issued at CLJN's sole discretion based on the circumstances presented.

  • Refund requests must be submitted through your account settings or via billing@cljn.com within the eligible window.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days and returned to the original payment method.
  • Annual plan refunds are calculated on a prorated basis less any sessions used, minus a $15 administrative processing fee.
  • Promotional, discounted, or bundle subscription purchases are non-refundable unless required by applicable consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction.

❌ Session Cancellation Policy

Individual consultation sessions may be cancelled or rescheduled without penalty up to 4 hours before the scheduled start time. Cancellations made within 4 hours of a session will forfeit the session credit. No-shows forfeit the session credit and may incur a $5 no-show fee on Pay-Per-Session bookings.

  • Session credits expire 90 days after the billing period in which they were included.
  • Minister-initiated cancellations result in automatic session credit restoration and a priority rebooking slot.
  • Technical failures caused by platform infrastructure will be reviewed and compensated with replacement session credits within 24 hours.
🚫
Failed Payment Handling: If a payment fails, we will attempt to process the charge up to 3 times over a 7-day period. After the final failed attempt, your account will be suspended. Access will be restored immediately upon successful payment. We recommend keeping your payment details current to avoid service interruptions.

Section 06

Consultation Rules & Disclaimer

⚠️ Spiritual Guidance Disclaimer

Consultations and guidance provided by ministers on the CLJN platform are intended exclusively for spiritual, pastoral, and faith-based support. Nothing communicated during a CLJN session constitutes, or should be construed as, professional medical advice, psychological therapy, legal counsel, financial planning, or crisis intervention.

CLJN ministers are spiritual advisors, not licensed therapists, physicians, attorneys, or financial advisors. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, mental health crisis, legal matter, or financial hardship requiring professional intervention, please contact the appropriate licensed professionals and emergency services immediately.

🆘
Emergency Services: If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact emergency services (911 in the US) or the National Crisis Hotline (988) immediately. CLJN ministers are not equipped to provide emergency response services.

🎙 Session Recording Policy

To protect the privacy and spiritual intimacy of all consultation sessions, recording of any kind — including screen capture, audio recording, or transcription via third-party tools — is strictly prohibited without the express written consent of both the minister and CLJN.

  • CLJN may, with user consent, record sessions for quality assurance, minister training, and platform improvement purposes.
  • Users who consent to session recording will be notified at the start of each recorded session and may revoke consent at any time.
  • Session recordings retained by CLJN are stored in encrypted format for no more than 90 days, after which they are permanently deleted.
  • Unauthorized recording of sessions may result in immediate account termination and potential legal action under applicable wiretapping and privacy laws.

📋 User Responsibility Notice

You acknowledge that spiritual guidance is deeply personal and subjective in nature. CLJN does not guarantee any specific spiritual outcome, transformation, or result from consultations. Your spiritual journey remains your personal responsibility, and the platform's role is solely to facilitate connection with qualified spiritual advisors.

🛡 Encrypted Payment Processing

CLJN partners exclusively with Stripe, a globally recognized PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment processor, to handle all financial transactions. CLJN never stores, processes, or transmits your complete credit card number, CVV, or banking details on our own servers.

  • All payment data is tokenized at the point of entry and transmitted directly to Stripe's secure vaults via TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Stored payment methods are represented solely by secure tokens — your actual card details are never accessible to CLJN employees.
  • Suspicious transactions are flagged by our real-time fraud detection system powered by Stripe Radar, and potentially compromised accounts are immediately locked pending verification.
  • Transaction receipts and billing history are retained securely for 7 years in compliance with financial record-keeping regulations.
