✦ Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration free on CLJN? +

Yes, creating a CLJN account is completely free. You can access selected community features and browse minister profiles at no cost. Premium subscriptions unlock the full library, live consultations, and exclusive content.

Can I become a minister after registering as a subscriber? +

Absolutely. You may apply to become a verified minister from your dashboard at any time. Our team reviews credentials, spiritual background, and references before granting minister status — a process that takes 3–7 business days.

Is my personal information and spiritual data secure? +

Your privacy is our highest priority. All data is encrypted using AES-256 standards, sessions are end-to-end encrypted, and we are fully GDPR compliant. We never sell, share, or misuse your personal information.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time? +

Yes. You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings with no cancellation fees. You retain access to premium features until the end of your current billing period.

Is CLJN available worldwide? +

Yes! CLJN is accessible in 35+ countries with localized payment options, multilingual support, and regionally curated spiritual content. We are continuously expanding our reach to serve more nations.
