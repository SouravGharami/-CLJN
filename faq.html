How do consultations with ministers work? Popular ⌄

CLJN consultations are real-time sacred conversations between you and a verified spiritual minister. The process is simple and deeply intentional:

  • Browse our community of 1,800+ verified ministers by specialty — prayer, counseling, deliverance, prophecy, or general spiritual guidance.
  • Choose a session format: Live Video, Voice Call, or Sacred Chat.
  • Select a time that aligns with your schedule from the minister's live calendar.
  • Complete a brief pre-session intention form to help your minister prepare spiritually.
  • Receive a secure session link via email. No downloads required — sessions run in your browser.

Payments are held securely in escrow and only released to the minister after the session is marked complete to your satisfaction.

Are my chat consultations private and secure? Privacy ⌄

Your spiritual conversations are held in complete sacred confidence. CLJN employs enterprise-grade security across every session:

  • End-to-end encryption on all chat, voice, and video sessions — not even CLJN staff can access session content.
  • Session transcripts are stored in your private encrypted vault, accessible only to you.
  • Ministers are bound by our Spiritual Confidentiality Agreement and community covenant.
  • You may delete session records permanently at any time from your account.

We are fully GDPR and CCPA compliant. Your journey remains between you, your minister, and God.

How are ministers verified on CLJN? Trust ⌄

Every minister on CLJN passes our Sacred Verification Program — one of the most rigorous in the digital ministry space:

  • Credentialing check: Ordination certificates and ministerial credentials verified against issuing organizations.
  • Identity verification: Government-issued ID confirmed via our secure KYC partner.
  • Background screening: National criminal background check conducted.
  • Theological assessment: Ministers complete a doctrinal alignment interview.
  • Community review: Minimum 30-day monitored onboarding period before full listing.

Verified ministers display the ✦ Sacred Seal badge on their profile. We remove ministers who violate our covenant — zero tolerance for spiritual misconduct.

What subscription plans does CLJN offer? Popular ⌄

CLJN offers three sacred membership tiers to meet every believer where they are:

  • Seeker (Free) — Browse minister profiles, access community prayer forums, attend one free group session per month.
  • Devotee ($19/month) — 3 private sessions per month, session recordings saved to your vault, priority support queue, access to archived sermons and teachings.
  • Oracle ($49/month) — Unlimited sessions, exclusive access to senior apostolic ministers, monthly group prophetic gatherings, early access to new features, and a dedicated spiritual care coordinator.

Annual billing is available at a 25% discount. All plans include a 14-day free trial — no card required for Seeker. Devotee and Oracle include a 7-day money-back guarantee.

💰
What is the refund and cancellation policy? Important ⌄

We believe every sacred investment should feel risk-free. Our policy is designed to honor both your resources and the minister's time:

  • Full refund: Cancel or reschedule any session more than 24 hours before the scheduled start.
  • 50% refund or credit: Cancellations made within 24 hours of the session receive a half-refund or full credit toward a future booking.
  • No-show policy: No-shows are non-refundable out of respect for the minister's reserved time.
  • Technical issues: If a session is interrupted by platform issues on our end, you receive an automatic full refund within 2 business days.
  • Subscription refunds: Pro-rated refunds available within the first 7 days of a billing cycle.

Disputes are handled by our pastoral care team within 48 hours. We always err on the side of grace.

What payment methods are accepted? ⌄

CLJN accepts a wide range of secure payment methods to make your experience seamless:

  • Credit & Debit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
  • Digital Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal
  • Bank Transfer: ACH direct debit (US accounts)
  • Ministry Giving Credits: Redeemable gift codes from partner ministries

All transactions are processed via Stripe — PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and never store raw card details on our servers. Subscriptions auto-renew on your billing date; you'll receive a 3-day advance reminder email before each charge.

👤 Account & Privacy
⚙️
How do I manage my account settings and profile? ⌄

Your CLJN profile is your spiritual home base. Here's how to keep it aligned with your journey:

  • Navigate to Dashboard → Profile to update your display name, profile photo, spiritual interests, and bio.
  • Under Settings → Security, change your password or enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via authenticator app or SMS.
  • Adjust notification preferences — session reminders, community updates, and prophetic newsletters — under Settings → Notifications.
  • Set your timezone for accurate session scheduling across all global ministers.
  • Account deletion is available under Settings → Privacy → Close Account. Requests are processed within 30 days per GDPR requirements, with full data export available.
✦ Account
🛡️
How is my personal and spiritual data protected? ⌄

CLJN treats your data as sacred. We are fully GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA-adjacent compliant:

  • All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Session transcripts stored in your personal encrypted vault — ministers cannot re-access content post-session.
  • We never sell, share, or monetize your personal or spiritual data to third parties.
  • Request a complete data export or permanent deletion at any time from your Privacy Settings.
  • Third-party integrations (payment processors, email providers) are vetted against our data covenant.

Our full Privacy Policy is available at cljn.com/privacy. Questions? Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at dpo@cljn.com.
