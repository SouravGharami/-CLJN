Biography
About the Minister
A life surrendered to divine purpose and spiritual excellence

Prophet Emmanuel Osei was born in Kumasi, Ghana, in 1975, into a family deeply rooted in Christian faith. From a young age, he demonstrated an exceptional sensitivity to spiritual matters, and by age 16, he had already begun preaching in his local church to considerable acclaim.

After completing his theological studies at the West Africa Seminary of Theology, Prophet Osei launched his itinerant ministry in 1999. Within the first decade, he had ministered in over 47 countries across Africa, Europe, and the Americas, with documented miracles, healings, and prophetic utterances that have transformed countless lives.

He founded the Tabernacle of Grace International Ministry in 2005, which today operates 38 branches across 12 nations, with a global online congregation exceeding 500,000 followers. His signature "Fire and Glory" crusades have attracted crowds of up to 80,000 attendees.

Prophet Osei holds a Master's degree in Theology from Oxford Centre for Mission Studies and a Doctorate in Ministry from Regent University, Virginia. He is also a prolific author with 14 published books on prayer, healing, and prophetic ministry.

Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know before booking
How does the consultation process work?
After booking your session, you will receive a confirmation email with a secure video call link. At your scheduled time, simply join the link and connect with Prophet Osei directly. Sessions are conducted via secure video conferencing, and recordings are provided for Premium and Mentorship packages.
What is your refund and cancellation policy?
You may reschedule or cancel your session up to 24 hours before the appointment for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours of the session are eligible for a 50% refund or credit toward a future session. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time with no future charges.
How do I know when Prophet Osei is available?
The booking calendar above displays real-time availability. Slots marked in gold are open for booking, while greyed-out slots are already reserved. Prophet Osei is generally available Monday through Friday, with limited weekend availability for emergency consultations.
What does a monthly subscription include?
Monthly subscribers receive priority booking privileges, discounted session rates, access to exclusive teaching content, a monthly personal prophetic word, and inclusion in the Prophet's personal prayer list. Mentorship subscribers additionally receive 24/7 chat access and weekly sessions.
Is my personal information kept confidential?
Absolutely. All sessions are strictly confidential. Personal information shared during consultations is never disclosed to third parties. Our platform is GDPR compliant and uses end-to-end encryption for all communications and session recordings.
