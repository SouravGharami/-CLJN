✦ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply to become a CLJN minister?
+
Any ordained or recognized spiritual leader can apply — including pastors, prophets, evangelists, apostles, teachers, worship ministers, and spiritual counsellors. We welcome ministers from all Christian denominations and backgrounds across the globe.
How long does the verification process take?
+
The standard verification process takes 3–5 business days from the time we receive a complete application with all required documents. You'll receive real-time email updates at each stage of the review process.
Is there any joining or subscription fee?
+
Joining CLJN as a minister is completely free. We operate on a revenue-sharing model — we earn only when you earn. A small platform commission (10–15%) is applied on transactions, but there are no upfront fees or monthly charges.
How do ministers earn on CLJN?
+
Ministers earn through multiple streams: monthly subscriptions, one-on-one consultations, premium mentorship programs, exclusive teachings/course sales, and member donations. Payments are processed securely and withdrawn to your local bank account or digital wallet.
Can I upload video sermons and teachings after registration?
+
Absolutely. Once your profile is approved, you'll have access to our media management dashboard where you can upload unlimited video sermons, audio teachings, written devotionals, and PDFs. Content can be set as free or premium.
Is CLJN available globally?
+
Yes — CLJN is a fully global platform. We currently serve ministers and members in 35+ countries across Africa, Europe, North America, Asia, and beyond. The platform supports multiple languages and currencies for a seamless international experience.
