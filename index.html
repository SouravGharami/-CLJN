Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CLJN.

How are ministers verified on CLJN? +
All ministers go through a rigorous multi-step verification process including credential checks, background verification, reference reviews, and a theological assessment. We also monitor ongoing reviews and ratings from the community to maintain quality standards.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time? +
Absolutely. You have complete control over your subscriptions. You can cancel, pause, or switch ministers at any time from your dashboard with no penalty fees. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.
What denominations are represented on the platform? +
CLJN is interdenominational and welcomes ministers from Baptist, Pentecostal, Catholic, Anglican, Methodist, Charismatic, Non-denominational, and many other Christian traditions. We celebrate diversity in the Body of Christ.
Is my personal information and sessions kept confidential? +
Yes, completely. All sessions are private and encrypted. We have a strict no-sharing policy with third parties. Ministers are bound by a confidentiality covenant. Your spiritual journey is deeply personal and we treat it as such.
How do I become a minister on CLJN? +
Apply through our "Become a Minister" portal. Submit your credentials, ministry details, and references. Our team reviews applications within 5-7 business days. Once approved, you'll receive onboarding support and tools to launch your digital ministry.
Are sessions available in languages other than English? +
Yes! We have ministers who conduct sessions in over 35 languages including Spanish, French, Portuguese, Swahili, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Hindi, and many more. Use the language filter when searching for ministers to find your match.
