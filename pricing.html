Questions & Answers

Everything You Need
to Know

How do subscriptions work on CLJN?
+
When you subscribe to a plan or an individual minister, your payment is processed securely and access is granted immediately. Monthly subscriptions renew on the same date each month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your dashboard.
What is your refund policy?
+
We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee on all plan subscriptions. If you are not satisfied within the first 7 days, contact our support team for a full refund. Individual session bookings are non-refundable once the session has occurred, but can be rescheduled with 24-hour notice.
How do I book a session with a minister?
+
Navigate to any minister's profile and click "Book Session." You'll select an available time slot from their calendar, confirm the session type (audio, video, or chat), and complete payment. You'll receive a confirmation email with a session link.
Is my payment information secure?
+
Absolutely. We use 256-bit SSL encryption and are fully PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. We never store your card details on our servers. All payments are processed through Stripe, PayPal, or local mobile money providers — all industry-leading secure payment platforms.
Can I subscribe to individual ministers?
+
Yes! Beyond our platform plans, you can subscribe directly to any minister of your choosing. Individual minister subscriptions give you unlimited messaging access with that minister, exclusive content, and discounted session rates. This is separate from your platform plan.
How do I cancel my subscription?
+
You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings → Subscriptions in your dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, so you retain full access until then. There are no cancellation fees or penalties whatsoever.
