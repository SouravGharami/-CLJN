Common Questions

Answers to what our community asks most

How do I submit a prayer request to a specific minister?
▾
Navigate to any minister's profile page and tap the "Send Prayer Request" button. Your request will be delivered directly to their inbox and included in their personal prayer session. All requests are treated with complete confidentiality and reverence.
How do I access sermons offline on the CLJN app?
▾
Premium subscribers can download any sermon for offline listening. Simply tap the ⬇ download icon on any sermon card. Downloads are stored on your device and accessible via the "Downloaded" tab in My Library — even without internet access.
Can ministers apply to be featured on CLJN?
▾
Yes! Ministers and preachers are welcome to apply through our Ministry Partnership program. Select "Partnership & Outreach" in the contact form above, and our team will review your application and respond within 5 business days with onboarding information.
How do I report inappropriate content or a technical issue?
▾
Use the "Report an Issue" option in the contact form, or tap the flag icon directly on any piece of content. For urgent technical matters, our live chat is the fastest route to resolution — our agents typically respond in under 3 minutes.
Is CLJN free to use, and what does Premium include?
▾
CLJN is free for all core features — sermons, articles, and community access. Premium (from $4.99/month) adds offline downloads, ad-free listening, exclusive minister Q&A sessions, early access to new sermons, and priority prayer support.
